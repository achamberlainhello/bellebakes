# bellebakes
Sourdough and cookie orders on Stanford campus.

- `index.html` – bakery homepage with this week's menu and a "Place your order" button that opens Annabelle's Google Form.
- `coaching.html` – life coaching page, linking to the coaching site.

## Setup (in `index.html`)
- `ORDER_FORM_URL` – the Google Form link.
- `STOCK_CSV_URL` – the Sheet's "Stock" tab published as CSV. That tab holds only counts, never customer details.
- `VENMO_HANDLE` – her Venmo business profile, without the @.
